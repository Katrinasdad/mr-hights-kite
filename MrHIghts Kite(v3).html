Mr. Hight's Kite

by H. Allen Hight

scroll ↓

There once was a man named Mr. Hight, who decided to go fly a kite.

So he bought some paper, some wood, and some string, just about most everything.

But, on this day there was no wind.

So he sat on the porch waiting for the wind to begin.  He sat one full day and one full night, and he prayed for the wind with all his might.

The next morning about ten, he saw a rooster chasing a hen

He saw a girl on a bike

And he saw a boy named Mike

But… not a breath of wind

The next day he saw the dawning sun, and thought it would be really fun, to walk up to the hill so high, and have a talk with the morning sky.

“OK sky so red and blue,” he said, “I want to be like my older cousin Ned.” “He once made a big yellow six foot kite; he called it ‘Ned Hight’s first light kite.’”

“He lives in the city now,”
“But I still take care of his old brown cow.”

Just then a leaf moved on the ground.

And in a minute, more leaves moved all around.

Mr. Hight ran back down the hill, past the old brown down mill, by the creek, dry now from summer’s scorch.

His kite still lay near the rocker on his front porch.

Back to the top of the hill he ran, away from the trees, Mr. Hight’s kite strained to fly, and out it went… try… try… try… try

“Try to fly very high,” said Mr. Hight

And the kite flew happily, very high.  And if the kites could talk this one would have said, “Be happy, young ones, and look for me, old Ned, and Mr. Hight along the way…”

H. Allen Hight
